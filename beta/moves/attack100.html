On shield is frame advantage: −7 means the defender acts seven frames before you, so anything faster than that punishes. Aerials are measured against landing lag and assume you land on the hitting frame; everything else against the move's first actionable frame. Throws and command grabs show no figure, because those hits land on an opponent already held and never meet a shield.
